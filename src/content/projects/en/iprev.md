Interoperabilidad con Recaudo Electrónico Vehicular (IP/REV) is the national scheme that makes electronic toll payment work across operators. I was part of the team that built it: reactive, event-driven microservices designed for high concurrency and transaction volume, with TDD and code review as the working norm.
